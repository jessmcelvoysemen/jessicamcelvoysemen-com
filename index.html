Founder, UtilityFinder LLC

I'm a full-stack developer based in Indiana. I founded UtilityFinder LLC to answer a question that should have a simple answer but doesn't: who provides electric, gas, water, sewer, trash, and internet at a given U.S. address. UtilityFinder serves movers, homebuyers, realtors, and property managers, and is available at utilityfinder.com and on the iOS App Store.

I'm a Lead Data Product Owner at ACT by day, with a decade of product and data-architecture work behind me at Eli Lilly (via Onebridge), ADESA, and KAR Global. I hold an MBA from Purdue University Global.

I'm also a co-founder of Ruby & Rosie's, a dog wash opening in central Indiana in early 2027, where I'm building the software and infrastructure. Otherwise, I'm with my husband, our two kids, and our dog.
